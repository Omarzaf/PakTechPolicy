## Status decisions and unresolved questions

1. **Data Governance Policy 2026:** The PDF uses mandatory and binding language, but MoITT's current policy catalog labels the instrument `Draft`. The record therefore uses `Draft`. Confirm a Cabinet approval or Gazette notification before changing it to `In force`.

2. **SECP Cloud Adoption Guidelines:** The PDF contains an approval record and says the guidelines would become effective on 1 June 2021, but SECP's current document page, guidelines catalog, and announcement continue to call the instrument a draft. The record therefore uses `Draft`. Confirm whether a final version was formally issued before changing its status.

3. **5G documents:** MoITT's current policy catalog still labels both the Readiness Plan and the Strategic Plan/Policy Guidelines as drafts. Their original milestones are historical and should not be treated as evidence that the proposed actions occurred.

4. **5G Readiness Plan attribution:** The plan was prepared with World Bank technical assistance and expressly says its opinions and recommendations do not necessarily reflect the views of the Government of Pakistan, PTA, or FAB. The record treats it as an official MoITT-hosted draft framework, not an adopted government position.

5. **Digital Pakistan Policy:** MoITT's current policy catalog labels the 2018 policy `Approved`, so the record uses `In force`. The catalog does not identify an amendment or express replacement by a later policy.

6. **WASL maturity:** The Pakistan Digital Authority labels version 1.0 `Published (PUB)` and `PILOT` maturity. The record uses `In force` to represent its currently published status, while the summary preserves the pilot qualification.

7. **Cloud procurement document:** The official MoITT PDF is image-based, so its preface and structure were cross-checked against MoITT's current approved-policy listing and PPRA's standard-bidding-document index.

8. **No legal advice:** Summaries describe the official texts and their stated scope. They do not assess enforceability, compliance, constitutional validity, or rights and liabilities in a particular case.

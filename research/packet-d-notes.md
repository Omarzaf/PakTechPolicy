## Status decisions and unresolved questions

1. **e-Commerce Policy of Pakistan:** Official sources establish Cabinet approval on 1 October 2019, but the Ministry describes it as the `2019-24` policy and was still finalizing a replacement policy in July 2026. The record is therefore `Unverified`, and no continuing legal or policy effect is inferred.

2. **EMI Regulations:** The record uses the original 1 April 2019 issuance date, `Amended` status, and the 21 June 2023 revision date. SBP's current licensed-entities page continues to point users to these regulations.

3. **Federal RTI Rules date:** The official document is titled `Right of Access to Information Rules, 2019`, but the notification printed in the PDF is dated 5 May 2020. The record uses the printed notification date.

4. **KP RTI amendments:** The consolidated official text records substitutions made by Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Act No. XXIV of 2015. Because the checked source did not state the precise amendment day, `last_amended` uses the schema-required year-only placeholder `2015-01-01`.

5. **Sindh RTI date:** The title uses 2016, but the official Sindh Code identifies the instrument as Sindh Act No. XV of 2017 and publishes it under 12 April 2017. The record uses that official publication date.

6. **Balochistan Act numbering:** The Provincial Assembly identifies the RTI statute as Act No. II of 2021. The later official rules PDF appears to cite `Act No. 11 of 2021`. The packet follows the Assembly entry and preserves the discrepancy rather than silently resolving it.

7. **Balochistan implementation:** The existence of official rules and a current government document listing supports `In force`; the packet does not claim that the provincial information commission or every operational mechanism is fully functioning.

8. **No legal advice:** The summaries describe the official texts and their stated scope. They do not assess constitutional validity, enforceability in a particular case, compliance, or individual rights and liabilities.

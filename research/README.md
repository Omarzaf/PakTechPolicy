# Research packets

Each packet writes:

- `packet-<letter>.json` — records matching `docs/content-schema.md`
- `packet-<letter>-notes.md` — sources checked, unresolved questions, and any
  records deliberately marked `Unverified`

Packets:

- A: speech, rights, privacy, and internet access
- B: telecom regulation and cybersecurity
- C: digital strategy, cloud, and AI
- D: fintech, IT exports, e-commerce, and access to information

Research output is provisional until it passes consolidation and source QA.

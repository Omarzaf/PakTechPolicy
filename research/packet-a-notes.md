## Status and date decisions

- The consolidated PECA text was treated as the current statutory text because it
  incorporates both the 2023 and 2025 acts and is hosted by Pakistan Code.
- Both 2020 online-content rulesets are `Repealed` because rule 16 of the 2021
  rules expressly repeals them.
- The 2021 online-content rules are `In force`: the current Ministry rules index
  lists them, the Ministry yearbook records their gazette notification, and PECA
  2025 preserves earlier rules to the extent they are not inconsistent with the
  amended Act.
- The Personal Data Protection Bill is `Draft` because the current Ministry draft
  legislation page continues to label it as a draft. The source specifies May 2023
  but no day; `2023-05-01` uses the first day of the known month as a precision
  placeholder.
- The Electronic Transactions Ordinance is `Amended`, not repealed. Its former
  sections 36 and 37 were omitted by PECA, while the remaining electronic
  transactions and certification framework continues in the official text.
- The Fair Trial Act primary text gives 20 February 2013, while the Pakistan Code
  index gives a promulgation date of 22 February 2013. The packet uses the date
  printed in the Act.
- The Telecommunications Act primary text gives 13 October 1996, while one
  Pakistan Code index view reports 1 December 1996. The packet uses the date
  printed in the Act. Its 2024 amendment date is supported by Act No. XXV of 2024
  in the consolidated text and the National Assembly act listing.
- The Right of Way directive uses its notification date, 9 October 2020. It was
  published in the extraordinary gazette dated 31 December 2020.
- The 2006 USF rules are `Amended`; the official consolidated copy was checked
  together with the separate 2023 amendment gazette.

## Unverified record and unresolved questions

- **National Broadband Policy 2021, Consultation Draft v3.0** is deliberately
  `Unverified` for both `status` and `verification`. The official draft was opened,
  but no official adoption, withdrawal, or supersession notice was found in the
  current Ministry policy index. Consolidation QA should check the gazette,
  Cabinet/ECC records, or a later Ministry notification before assigning a current
  legal or policy status.
- The 2021 online-content PDF is stored under a filename containing “Final Draft”
  and leaves the S.R.O. number/date blank on its first page. Its identity and
  notification date were corroborated using the Ministry's current rules index and
  2021–22 yearbook; consolidation QA may replace the URL with a complete gazette
  scan if one is located.
- The Pakistan Code copy of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance still displays
  the original omitted offence sections. The packet relies on the later,
  consolidated PECA section 54 for their omission and avoids describing those
  offences as current ETO provisions.
- This packet does not attempt a province-by-province inventory of cybercrime,
  privacy, surveillance, or access instruments. It is limited to the federal
  instruments most directly relevant to the three assigned domains.

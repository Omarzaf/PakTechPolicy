Editorial method

Source first. Caution where certainty ends.

The index is a research aid, not legal advice. Records are written from official instruments and neutral supporting material. A green verification mark means the linked primary source was opened during the latest review; it does not guarantee that no later amendment, judgment, or notification exists.

  1. 01 Locate the official instrument
  2. 02 Record scope, dates, issuer, and relationships
  3. 03 Write a neutral plain-language summary
  4. 04 Flag unresolved status or source gaps

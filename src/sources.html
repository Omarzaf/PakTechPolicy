How to compare responsibly

  1. 01
    Check the period.

    Publication year and reference year often differ.

  2. 02
    Read the unit.

    Accounts, subscriptions, devices, and people are not interchangeable.

  3. 03
    Inspect the method.

    Composite indices, surveys, administrative records, and modelled estimates answer different questions.

  4. 04
    Keep gaps visible.

    Missing observations are not zero, and a reachable link is not proof of completeness.

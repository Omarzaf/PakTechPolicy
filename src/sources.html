How to compare responsibly

  1. 01
    Check the period.

    Publication year and reference year often differ.

  2. 02
    Read the unit.

    Accounts, subscriptions, devices, and people are not interchangeable.

  3. 03
    Inspect the method.

    Composite indices, surveys, administrative records, and modelled estimates answer different questions.

  4. 04
    Keep gaps visible.

    Missing observations are not zero, and a reachable link is not proof of completeness.

Public trust

Sources are evidence—not endorsements.

This independent, open-source research project is maintained by Muhammad Umar Zafar. Listed institutions neither sponsor nor endorse the index; their marks identify the official publishers being cited.

01

Editorial independence

Publisher inclusion reflects relevance to the research question, not affiliation. No commissioned or sponsored relationship is represented by this release.

02

Review cadence

Link checks and evidence review happen in dated release snapshots, not through continuous monitoring. Access limitations stay visible.

03

Privacy by design

This static site uses no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. External source sites have their own privacy practices.

04

Challenge or improve a source

Cite the affected resource and official evidence. GitHub requires an account; submissions are public, so do not include sensitive information.
